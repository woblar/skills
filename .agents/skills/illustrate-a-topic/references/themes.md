# The ten layout themes

Use the numbers, titles, or canonical names below. Keep the order stable for an all-ten set. The example links show composition and style.

| # | Theme | Canonical name | Composition | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pipeline | `pipeline` | One connected pipe or channel with 3–5 chambers showing a transformation. Use a landscape format. | [Agent runtime](../assets/examples/agents/01-pipeline.jpg) |
| 2 | Comparison grid | `comparison-grid` | A single labelled matrix with small icons. Compare responsibilities, parts, or properties. Use a landscape format. | [FEM and FEA](../assets/examples/fem/02-comparison-grid.jpg) |
| 3 | Four-frame comic | `four-frame-comic` | Four illustrated moments across one row, with stick people, short dialogue, and clear reading order. Use a wide landscape format. | [Patio heater](../assets/examples/heater/03-four-frame-comic.jpg) |
| 4 | Annotated object | `annotated-object` | One large object or conceptual cutaway, with 4–7 leader annotations and an optional magnified detail. Fit the format to the object. | [Patio heater](../assets/examples/heater/04-annotated-object.jpg) |
| 5 | Vertical workflow | `vertical-workflow` | A top-to-bottom stack of 4–6 wide bars with icons, short explanations, and arrows showing the actual sequence. Use a portrait format. | [Agent task](../assets/examples/agents/05-vertical-workflow.jpg) |
| 6 | Direct comparison | `direct-comparison` | Two illustrated scenes with contrasting captions. Show how the sides relate. Use “vs” for alternatives and “uses” or “complements” where appropriate. Use a landscape format. | [FEM and FEA](../assets/examples/fem/06-direct-comparison.jpg) |
| 7 | Bold takeaway poster | `bold-takeaway` | A short oversized headline, one dominant illustration, and 2–3 supporting callouts. Use a landscape format. | [Patio heater](../assets/examples/heater/07-bold-takeaway.jpg) |
| 8 | Letter framework | `letter-framework` | Three or four large mnemonic letters in pastel columns, each with a memorable concept and icon. Identify invented mnemonics as learning aids. Use a landscape format. | [FEM checks](../assets/examples/fem/08-letter-framework.jpg) |
| 9 | Numbered process | `numbered-process` | Four numbered illustrated cards in a 2×2 sequence with verb-led captions. Use a landscape format. | [Shelf analysis](../assets/examples/fem/09-numbered-process.jpg) |
| 10 | Visual progression | `visual-progression` | Three comparable open scenes showing the same subject at successive states, levels, or arrangements. Keep relevant conditions consistent. Use a landscape format. | [Patio heater](../assets/examples/heater/10-visual-progression.jpg) |

The `assets/examples/` directory also has full sets for agent runtimes (`agents/`), finite element analysis (`fem/`), and an LPG patio heater (`heater/`). Files are numbered in canonical order. Choose an alternate when it is a closer match for the subject or composition. Check the written layout first when an example mixes patterns. For instance, the heater pipeline uses separate cards and its grid includes a cutaway.
